How PRIDE Lead works · Marketplace model

PRIDE Lead connects borrowers who need credit to licensed financiers. Funds move through an escrow account. Platform fee: 5% from the borrower and 5% from the financier on successful disbursement. Underwriting is cashflow-first — we do not lend primarily on the strength of security.

Kiburi — Credit Scout Scores using Pochi la Biashara, Till, Bank, Paybill (primary) and M-Pesa (secondary). Flags M-Pesa outliers. Runs conversion / cashflow analysis.
Hekima — Risk Guardian SASRA, Kenya DPA 2019, CRB, bias GUARD, briefcase-company checks, KYC completeness.
Uamuzi — Human Liaison Officer briefing in English / Swahili / Dholuo. Final decision stays with the licensed officer.

Low-data / offline: Core forms work on slow networks. Production roadmap includes USSD for feature phones and offline draft save.

Consent · Data protection · Terms
Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 · Consent

Before we assess your application, you must consent to processing of your personal and financial data. PRIDE Lead is designed to comply with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and related regulations.

Summary of what we process

• Identity data: name, National ID/Passport, photo, KRA PIN, phone
• Business data: location, registration, CR12, incorporation certificates where applicable
• Financial data: Pochi la Biashara, Till, Paybill, bank statements, M-Pesa statements, chama journals
• Credit data: CRB records (pulled only with your consent through licensed bureaux)
• Call-report referee details for verification

Purpose
Credit assessment, fraud and briefcase-company controls, matching you to a licensed financier, escrow settlement, audit trail, and regulatory reporting where required.

Your rights
Access, correction, deletion (subject to legal retention), restriction, and complaint to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC). You may withdraw consent; processing already done lawfully may remain for audit/compliance.

Sharing
Only with: (1) licensed financiers you are matched to, (2) licensed CRB providers under consent, (3) regulators when legally required. We do not sell your data.

Security
Encryption in transit, access controls, human-in-the-loop for final credit decisions, and retention limited to what the law and credit lifecycle require.

All four consents are required before running agent decisioning.

Key terms (short)
  • PRIDE Lead is a marketplace connecting borrowers to licensed financiers; it is not itself a deposit-taking institution.
  • Final credit decision always rests with a licensed human officer / financier.
  • Platform fee: 5% of loan amount from the borrower and 5% from the financier on successful disbursement via escrow.
  • False information or synthetic briefcase companies may lead to decline, reporting, and legal action.
  • You may appeal declines through the published adverse-action path.

Borrower ↔ Financier marketplace
Business model
Successful disbursement: 5% fee from borrower + 5% fee from financier. Funds settle via escrow. Platform does not take principal risk.
Escrow & settlement

On officer approval and financier match, loan amount is held in escrow. Release to borrower only after KYC + call-report verification and signed offer.

Briefcase-company controls
  • Mandatory physical location + call-report referee
  • CR12 / incorporation for limited companies
  • Business KRA PIN when registered
  • Primary cashflow (Pochi / Till / Bank) preferred over pure M-Pesa
  • M-Pesa outlier detection for officer questions
  • Cashflow conversion analysis — do not lend on security alone
  • Human officer final decision + audit trail
Conversion / cashflow principle
The system analyses what part of the business the loan finances (stock cycle, equipment, agri season). We underwrite cashflow, not collateral strength. Security may support recovery but must not be the primary reason to lend. Kiburi reports a simple conversion view (loan size vs. monthly primary cashflow) so the officer can see affordability clearly.
