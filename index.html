▶ How PRIDE Lead works · Agents · Guard rails · Why it matters for the panel

The problem

Jua Kali traders and SACCO members are often excluded by traditional credit scoring. Opaque models can embed bias; regulators (SASRA, ODPC) require explainability, consent, and human oversight for high-stakes decisions.

Three specialised agents + human officer

Kiburi — Credit Scout Alternative data: M-Pesa averages, chama tenure, trade type. Produces a transparent risk score and seals the payload.
Hekima — Risk Guardian SASRA capital adequacy, collateral, Kenya DPA consent. Can trigger a PRIDE Loop pause for Ethics Committee review.
Uamuzi — Human Liaison Generates a clear briefing in Swahili / Dholuo / English and delivers via Dashboard, WhatsApp or USSD.

PRIDE Loop & GUARD rail

Any gender-proxy bias detected in the scoring payload is blocked immediately (GUARD). SASRA breaches or missing DPA consent pause the workflow so a human can intervene before a decision is finalised. Every run writes an auditable trail.

Panel talking points

Explainability · Human-in-the-loop · Local language & low-literacy channels · Data residency in Africa · Right to appeal via USSD · Designed for regulatory sandboxes and responsible AI frameworks in Kenya.
